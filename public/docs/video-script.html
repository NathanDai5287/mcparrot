Keeping Every Claim Honest

Judges penalize polish that hides parts that do not work, so the video shows only real runs and says nothing the status guide does not support. DeepSeek appears only on the hook slide as an example of a slow website; never say or show that MCParrot works with DeepSeek. Ordering is a gap: a Browser Use agent has filled a real DoorDash cart, but payment with Pay with Link runs only in test mode, so it has never paid for a real order. The DoorDash line says this plainly, and the video must never imply that MCParrot placed or paid for an order. In clip 4, check that the expanded Worked for list shows view_image; if it does not, say "the class stats from an Ed post" and drop the sentence about Claude reading the chart.

Blur every private value before export: passwords, the connection link, verification codes, the DoorDash delivery address, saved cards, email addresses, and other students' names. Never show the Browser Use, Clerk, or Sentry dashboards. Every sped-up wait carries a "sped up" label, so no viewer thinks the answers are instant.

Recording and Editing Tips

  • Record at 1920 × 1080, with the browser zoom at 125 percent so text stays readable on a phone.
  • Hide the bookmarks bar and desktop icons, and turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification appears.
  • Start the long ChatGPT runs first, clip 4 and clip 6, in separate tabs, and record the other clips while they work.
  • Keep the cursor still during waits, then speed-ramp them in the edit with a "sped up" label.
  • Record the voice separately with a headset or USB microphone in a quiet room, reading each line twice and keeping the better take.
  • Burn in captions, because many judges watch with the sound off.
  • Export as MP4 at 1080p, and watch it once on a phone before uploading.
